How it works

From hover to price in three steps

  1. 1

    Hover an item

    In your inventory, stash or a trade window, in the English PoE 1 client.

  2. 2

    Press the shortcut

    ⌃D on Mac, CtrlShiftD on Windows. Both can be changed in Settings.

  3. 3

    Read the price

    The overlay opens next to the item with the typical ask and the cheapest listings. Click back on the game to close it.

Trade requests, under control

Answer buyers without typing chat commands

  1. 1
    See who wants what

    On Mac, turn on trade watching in ExileWorth’s Trades tab. Incoming trade whispers then open the in-game Trades panel with the buyer, item, price and league.

  2. 2
    Respond with one click

    Invite, trade, thank, kick, or pick one of your prepared replies. One press sends one command.

  3. 3
    Finish and move on

    Jump to your hideout, complete the exchange, and mark the request done when you are ready for the next buyer.

Questions

ExileWorth FAQ

Does ExileWorth work on Mac and Windows?

Yes. The Mac app needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. The Windows build is a preview for Windows 11 x64.

Is ExileWorth free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no accounts, telemetry or paid tier.

Are the downloads signed?

The Mac build is signed and notarized by Apple. The Windows preview is unsigned and may trigger Microsoft Defender SmartScreen. Both downloads are on GitHub Releases.

What does ExileWorth send to the game and trade site?

It reads the public trade API within GGG's published rate limits. In game it sends the item-copy shortcut once per price check, and each trade button types one chat command. It never automates anything on its own.

Does it support Path of Exile 2?

Not yet. ExileWorth supports Path of Exile 1, including Covens of Umbra.
