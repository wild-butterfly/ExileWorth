Questions

ExileWorth FAQ

Does ExileWorth work on Mac?

Yes. ExileWorth is purpose-built for macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

Is ExileWorth free?

Yes. It is free and open source under the MIT license, with no accounts, telemetry or paid tier.

Are the downloads signed?

The Mac build is signed and notarized by Apple. The Windows preview is unsigned. Both are available from the official ExileWorth GitHub release.

Does it support Path of Exile 2?

ExileWorth supports Path of Exile 1, including Covens of Umbra.
