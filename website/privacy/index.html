Website privacy · Updated 19 November 2026

Optional website analytics

The ExileWorth desktop apps have no analytics or telemetry. This notice applies only to this website.

Your choice

We load Google Analytics only after you choose “Accept analytics”. Choosing “No thanks” keeps analytics off and does not affect downloads or the app. We remember this choice in your browser’s local storage. You can change it below or through “Analytics choices” in the footer.

What is measured

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page visits, general device and browser information, referral sources, and interactions such as download-link clicks. It uses analytics cookies, including a pseudonymous browser identifier, and processes network information to provide reports. We do not enable advertising personalization or Google signals.

A download click does not prove that a download completed, that the app was installed, or that someone used it. Visits from people who decline analytics or block tracking are not included.

Google and hosting

Analytics data is processed by Google. Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and Google’s privacy policy. Reports are used to understand website reach and which downloads visitors choose. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and release downloads are served by GitHub under GitHub’s privacy statement, independently of our analytics choice.

Questions

You can contact the maintainer through the ExileWorth GitHub project. The desktop app privacy statement describes the apps separately.
